## 5. Reddit r/sales, r/personalfinance, r/financialindependence — community protocol, NO pitch

ESPP and draw questions are weekly; I-bond threads spike every May/November rate
announcement. Strict norms:
- ESPP: the 15% discount with a lookback is the best risk-adjusted return most
  employees ever see — selling at purchase ("same-day sale") locks a ~17.6%
  gain on cost in 6 months and is NOT wrong just because it's disqualifying.
  Qualifying only helps if the stock holds up; in a down offering period it can
  cost more tax. The $25k limit is grant-date value — a doubling stock lets you
  buy $50k of shares.
- Commission draws: "recoverable" = loan. Breakeven = draw ÷ rate, every month.
  Ask about the termination clause BEFORE signing; deficit balances usually
  survive quitting. Non-recoverable is smaller for a reason.
- I bonds: rate resets lag — your bond changes 6 months from YOUR issue month,
  not on announcement day. Fixed rate is locked for 30 years; buying before a
  fixed-rate cut matters more than timing inflation. Composite floored at zero.
- 10+ substantive answers before any link; link only when asked "what did you
  calculate this with."
