## 5. Reddit r/jobs, r/careerguidance, r/smallbusiness, r/humanresources — community protocol, NO pitch

Exemption and offer threads are daily. Strict norms:
- The threshold question: current federal is $684/week ($35,568). $43,888 and
  $58,656 are the vacated 2024 rule — never in force past November 2024, and
  removed from the CFR May 15, 2026. State floors (CA ~$1,352/wk, WA
  ~$1,541.70/wk) override when higher.
- Salary alone never exempts: basis + level + duties, all three. Docking pay
  for slow weeks breaks salary basis (Helix: a $200k day-rate worker was
  non-exempt). Titles are legally irrelevant.
- Salaried non-exempt OT: salary ÷ actual hours = regular rate; half-time
  premium owed past 40. Claims typically add equal liquidated damages.
- Offer comparison: price match at face (mind vesting), health premiums at the
  employer's monthly contribution × 12, PTO at salary ÷ 260/day — then divide
  by REAL hours, not 40.
- Draws: recoverable = loan; breakeven = draw ÷ rate; read the termination
  clause before signing.
- 10+ substantive answers before any link; link only when asked "what did you
  calculate this with."
