## 5. Reddit r/Truckers, r/militaryfinance, r/Teachers, r/fednews — community protocol, NO pitch

Per-diem and TSP/403(b) threads are weekly in all four. Strict norms:
- Truckers: always ask owner-op vs W-2 first — the answer forks completely.
  Owner-ops: $80/day CONUS (Notice 2025-54), 75% partial days, 80% deductible,
  ELD logs are the substantiation. W-2 drivers CANNOT deduct per diem at all
  since the misc-deduction elimination went permanent — carrier per-diem pay is
  their only version, and it cuts SS/unemployment/loan-verifiable wages.
- Military: correct the "match vests" myth — your contributions and the MATCH are
  always yours; only the automatic 1% vests (2 yrs BRS / 3 yrs FERS). Front-loading
  loses match per pay period. Roth catch-up is mandatory in 2026 if prior-year SS
  wages > $150k. Dual-status (civilian + uniformed) share ONE elective deferral
  limit but get TWO matches.
- Teachers: the 15-year catch-up's third prong ($5k × years − lifetime deferrals)
  is the one that disqualifies heavy savers — and it stacks with age-50 in the
  mandatory order (15-year first). District moves reset the service clock; moves
  WITHIN a district generally don't.
- 10+ substantive answers before any link; link only when asked "what did you
  calculate this with."
