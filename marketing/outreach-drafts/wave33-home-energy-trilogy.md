## 5. Reddit r/solar, r/electricvehicles, r/heatpumps & r/homeowners — community protocol, NO pitch

Quote-check threads are the daily staple in all four subs. Strict norms:
- Solar quotes: first question is always cash price per watt BEFORE incentives
  ($2.50–3.50/W is the 2026 sane band); then net-metering terms; then warn that any
  federal credit line on a 2026 homeowner quote is either stale or the lease company's
  §48E.
- EV threads: ask where they charge before anything else. Home L2 → run the math;
  street parking → the honest answer is often "not yet." Mention state rebates still
  exist (several $1,500–$6,000).
- Heat pump threads: ask for their gas price per therm and electric rate — the answer
  flips on those two numbers. Cold climates: dual-fuel below the balance point is the
  standard recommendation, and "COP 2.8 seasonal" is a fair modern assumption.
- Never validate a specific installer/dealer quote as "good" — validate the math
  around it and name what to verify.
- 10+ substantive answers before any link; link only when asked "what did you run
  this in."
