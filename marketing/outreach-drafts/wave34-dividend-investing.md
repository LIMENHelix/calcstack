## 5. Reddit r/dividends, r/investing & r/personalfinance — community protocol, NO pitch

"Should I DRIP?" and "rate my dividend portfolio" threads are daily. Strict norms:
- DRIP questions: the accumulation-phase answer is almost always yes; the real
  decision point is retirement (take the cash as income) or the need to redirect
  dividends into underweight positions (the "poor man's rebalancing" move).
- Yield-on-cost threads: celebrate the metric but correct the misuse — YOC describes
  YOUR history with the stock; it doesn't make a low-current-yield hold automatically
  better than selling for a higher-yielder. Opportunity cost is the grown-up frame.
- Always ask taxable vs IRA before dividend-tax answers: reinvested dividends are
  taxed annually in taxable accounts; qualified vs ordinary rates differ; REIT and
  money-market dividends are ordinary income.
- Yield-trap calls: payout ratio over ~80% (or over 100% of FFO for REITs), rising
  debt, flat revenue — name the checklist, not just "too high."
- 10+ substantive answers before any link; link only when asked "what did you project
  this with."
