## 5. Reddit r/FirstTimeHomeBuyer & r/Mortgages — community protocol, NO pitch

Both subs are strict about self-promotion; r/Mortgages is working LOs who will shred a
bad calculator. Norms:
- Answer with the worked numbers inline. Payment questions get P&I computed and shown;
  "is this a good deal" questions get the break-even math, not an opinion.
- FHA threads: always separate UFMIP (1.75%, financed) from annual MIP (0.55%/typical,
  monthly) — the most common confusion. VA threads: ask about exemption and prior use
  before quoting the fee.
- Points threads: the honest answer is "divide cost by monthly savings; keep the loan
  past that many months or don't buy the point." Give the 60-month example.
- Rate quotes are time-sensitive — never validate a poster's quote, only the math
  around it.
- 10+ substantive answers before any link; link only when asked "what did you use to
  figure that."
