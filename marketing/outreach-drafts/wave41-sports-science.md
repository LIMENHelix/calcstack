## 5. Reddit r/running, r/advancedrunning, r/powerlifting, r/weightroom — community protocol, NO pitch

Race-prediction and scoring threads are weekly. Strict norms:
- Riegel: T₂ = T₁ × (D₂/D₁)^1.06 assumes equivalent training for both distances.
  A 5K predicts your marathon ONLY if you did the long runs. The formula breaks
  past the marathon — ultras are economy and fueling, not exponents. If your
  actuals beat predictions at long distances, you're endurance-gifted: train speed.
- Wilks vs DOTS: don't compare across systems. Wilks was retired for favoring
  heavier lifters; "my Wilks was 400" and "my DOTS is 400" are different claims.
  IPF GL is the IPF's own current system; DOTS is what USAPL/USPA best-lifter
  awards use.
- Periodization: anatomical adaptation is not wasted time — tendons adapt slower
  than muscle. Conversion (strength → power) is the phase self-coached athletes
  skip. Taper = cut volume ~50%, KEEP intensity. Power decays in ~2 weeks, so it
  trains last.
- 10+ substantive answers before any link; link only when asked "what did you
  calculate this with."
