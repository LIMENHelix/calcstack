## 5. Reddit r/personalfinance, r/financialindependence & r/tax — community protocol, NO pitch

Roth eligibility threads spike January–April. Strict norms:
- Answer with the formula applied: state their reduced limit in dollars, show the
  multiplication, mention the round-up-to-$10 and $200 floor (almost nobody knows
  these two rules — they are the highest-value part of the answer).
- Always ask filing status and MAGI (not salary — 401(k)/HSA contributions lower
  MAGI, which is itself the actionable tip for people near the band edge).
- Over-the-limit threads: backdoor Roth is the standard answer, but always flag the
  pro-rata rule — existing pre-tax IRA balances (rollovers from old 401(k)s) poison
  it, and the fix is rolling them into a current 401(k) first.
- Excess contribution threads: the fix is withdrawing the excess plus earnings
  before the filing deadline; the 6% penalty applies per year it sits.
- Deadline is the tax-filing deadline (mid-April), not December 31 — correct this
  gently whenever it appears.
- 10+ substantive answers before any link; link only when asked "what did you use
  to compute that."
