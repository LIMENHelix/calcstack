## 5. Reddit r/personalfinance, r/tax, r/selfhosted... r/financialindependence, r/whitecoatinvestor — community protocol, NO pitch

2026-rule threads are constant. Strict norms:
- QBI: thresholds $201,750 single / $403,500 MFJ (NOT the 24% bracket tops).
  Phase-in $75k/$150k (was $50k/$100k). New $400 minimum needs $1,000+ QBI and
  material participation; it does not rescue SSTBs past the top. Sole-prop QBI
  ≈ 85–93% of Schedule C profit (subtract SE-tax half, SE health, SEP/SIMPLE).
- AMT: phase-out starts $500k/$1M at the doubled 50% rate; exemption gone by
  $680,200/$1,280,400. 26% to $244,500 of excess, 28% above. ISO
  exercise-and-hold is the classic trigger; AMT on timing items becomes a
  Form 8801 credit.
- ACA: enhanced subsidies expired. 2.10%–9.96% reverted table, 400% cliff is
  back, APTC repayment caps gone. 2026 coverage uses Jan 2025 FPL ($15,650
  single contiguous). Under 100% FPL: Medicaid (expansion states) or the gap.
- EITC: max $664/$4,427/$7,316/$8,231; investment income over $12,200 zeroes
  it outright; phase-out measured on the GREATER of AGI or earnings; PATH Act
  refunds late February at the earliest.
- Saver's credit: 50/20/10% cliffs at $24,250/$26,250/$40,250 single
  ($48,500/$52,500/$80,500 MFJ); nonrefundable; Roth contributions count;
  2027 replaces it with the refundable Saver's Match paid into the account.
- 10+ substantive answers before any link; link only when asked "what did you
  calculate this with."
