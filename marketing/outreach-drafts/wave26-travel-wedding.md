## 5. Reddit r/travel, r/weddingplanning & r/personalfinance — community protocol, NO pitch

"How much should I budget for this trip/wedding" threads are daily in season.
Strict norms:
- Answer with the worked build inline — category by category, buffer included, ending
  at the monthly savings number.
- r/weddingplanning: the per-guest framing is welcome; judgment about spending levels
  is not. Give math, not opinions about what a wedding "should" cost.
- Peak season matters: wedding questions cluster Jan–Mar (engagement season), travel
  questions cluster Apr–Jun. Time participation accordingly.
- 10+ substantive answers before any link; link only when asked "what did you budget with."
