## 5. Reddit r/retirement, r/personalfinance, r/financialindependence, r/Medicare — community protocol, NO pitch

IRMAA and SS-taxation threads are weekly. Strict norms:
- IRMAA: 2026 premiums use 2024 MAGI against 2026 thresholds. First cliff
  $109,000 single / $218,000 joint; $1 over = $95.70/mo more for the year, PER
  PERSON. Part B standard $202.90; tiers up to $689.90; Part D adds $0–$91.00.
  Life-changing events (retirement, divorce, death of spouse) → Form SSA-44;
  voluntary Roth conversions do NOT qualify. Income at 63 sets premiums at 65.
- SS taxation: provisional income = AGI(before SS) + tax-exempt interest +
  half of benefits. $25k/$32k base, $34k/$44k second tier — statutory, frozen
  since 1984/1993. Muni interest counts; qualified Roth withdrawals do not.
  Inside the 85% zone, each $1 of income pulls $0.85 of benefits into taxable
  income: 22% bracket → 40.7% effective on withdrawals until the cap.
- IRA deduction: contributing ≠ deducting. Coverage decides the band, not just
  income. No workplace plan on either spouse → deductible at any income.
  Nondeductible contributions need Form 8606 or they get taxed twice.
