## 5. Reddit r/personalfinance, r/Banking & r/povertyfinance — community protocol, NO pitch

CD-vs-HYSA and "should I lock this up" threads recur with every rate move.
Strict norms:
- Answer with the worked math inline — maturity value, penalty in dollars, the HYSA
  comparison for the same period.
- Always mention the tax point: CD interest is ordinary income in the year credited,
  even before maturity — it changes the CD-vs-HYSA comparison not at all, but people
  get surprised.
- Rate environments change fast; never quote "current rates" from memory — tell posters
  to plug their actual offer into the math.
- 10+ substantive answers before any link; link only when asked "how did you run those numbers."
