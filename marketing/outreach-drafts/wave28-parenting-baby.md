## 5. Reddit r/personalfinance, r/beyondthebump & r/daddit — community protocol, NO pitch

"How much should we save before the baby" threads are weekly. Strict norms:
- Answer with the worked budget inline — childcare first, then the categories, ending
  at the monthly test-drive number.
- Localize: childcare ranges from $700 to $2,500+/month by metro; never quote a national
  average as the answer — tell posters to call two local daycares for real quotes.
- Mention the insurance move: adding a dependent changes premiums, and the birth year
  usually hits the deductible — it belongs in the medical line.
- 10+ substantive answers before any link; link only when asked "what did you budget with."
