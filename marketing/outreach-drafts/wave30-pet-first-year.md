## 5. Reddit r/dogs, r/cats, r/petinsurance & r/personalfinance — community protocol, NO pitch

"Can I afford a dog" threads are weekly in every pet sub. Strict norms:
- Answer with the worked budget inline — one-time costs first (adoption, initial vet,
  gear), then the run rate (food, vet, insurance, grooming), ending at the monthly
  number and the year-2+ figure.
- Localize: vet and insurance prices vary 2–3x by metro and breed; never quote a
  national average as the answer — tell posters to call two local vets for wellness
  exam and spay/neuter quotes.
- Insurance threads: give the honest trade (premium banked vs early major claim), note
  pre-existing condition exclusions and age-rising premiums, and never recommend for
  or against a specific insurer.
- Size matters: large-breed food and medication doses scale the budget; ask the breed
  before answering.
- 10+ substantive answers before any link; link only when asked "what did you budget with."
