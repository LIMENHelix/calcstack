## Rules

- Verify the embed renders on their platform BEFORE pitching (WordPress allows iframes in
  Custom HTML blocks; Medium does NOT — for Medium, pitch the link, not the embed).
- Never pitch the same niche leader twice in the same month.
- Every reply gets a same-day response. Speed is the differentiator vs. faceless tool sites.
- When an embed goes live, add the site to a "Featured on" section candidate list.
