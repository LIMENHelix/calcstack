# CalcStack — Marketing Playbook

Living document. Updated as we learn what works.

## Positioning

**What we are:** the calculator site that respects you. Every tool is instant,
free, and runs entirely in the browser. No email gates, no "sign up to see your
result," no cookie walls, no result pages hidden behind ads.

**Why that wins:** the incumbents (Calculator.net, Omni, bank-branded tools)
are ad-farms with 2002-era UX. Users tolerate them because nothing better
ranks. We beat them on speed, honesty, and design — then out-publish them
with programmatic SEO.

**Differentiators, in order of defensibility:**
1. **Privacy architecture** — calculations run client-side; literally nothing
   to leak. This is structural, not a promise competitors can copy without
   rebuilding.
2. **Tool-first page design** — calculator above the fold, results live on
   keystroke. Incumbents bury the tool under ads.
3. **Honest math content** — every page explains the formula and its traps
   (stacked discounts, annualized vs simple ROI, naive salary division).
   This is the content Google rewards and AI answers cite.
4. **Speed** — static site on Vercel edge, <100 KB gzipped JS. Core Web
   Vitals green by default.

## Channel plan

### 1. Programmatic SEO (primary engine, months 1–12)

The flywheel: more quality pages → more rankings → more traffic → more ad
and affiliate revenue → more pages.

- Phase 1 (done): 20 core calculators, each with unique copy + FAQ schema.
- Phase 2: **variant pages** — every calculator gets long-tail variants:
  "mortgage calculator with extra payments", "tip calculator for
  hairdressers", "BMI calculator for women over 50". Each is a new page from
  the same component with targeted copy. Target: 100 pages by month 3.
- Phase 3: **comparison/data pages** — "average mortgage payment by state",
  "freelance rates by profession" — data-driven pages that attract backlinks
  and AI citations.
- Phase 4: localize — VAT calculators per EU country, sales tax per US state
  (50 pages from one template), GBP/EUR currency display.

Rules we follow (learned from failed pSEO sites):
- Every page must have content that cannot be auto-generated by a competitor
  in one prompt — real explanations, real edge cases, real numbers.
- 500+ words of genuinely useful copy per page. No thin pages.
- Internal links: every page links to 3–5 siblings (already wired).

### 2. Search Console ops (owner task, 15 min/week)

- Verify site, submit sitemap (done: /sitemap.xml).
- Weekly: check "Pages" report for indexing issues; check "Queries" for
  keywords ranking 8–20 — those pages get copy improvements first.
- Rule: never delete a page that has impressions; improve it.

### 3. Community seeding (weeks 2–8)

- Reddit: answer real questions in r/personalfinance, r/freelance,
  r/financialindependence with the calculator as the worked example
  (link only when it genuinely answers the question — bans kill domains).
- Quora/StackExchange: same pattern for "how do I calculate X" questions.
- Product Hunt / Hacker News launch once we hit 30+ tools: "Calculator sites
  are ad farms, so I built one with no tracking." That story is the pitch.

### 4. Backlink magnets (months 2–6)

- **Embeddable widget**: iframe version of each calculator that bloggers can
  drop into articles, with a "Powered by CalcStack" link. Every embed is a
  backlink. This is how calculator sites actually scale authority.
- Data studies: "We analyzed 50,000 freelance rate calculations — median
  rate by profession." Journalists and bloggers link to original data.
- Free tool for a niche community: e.g. a cohort-based course pricing
  calculator for creators, an Etsy-fee calculator for sellers.

### 5. AI-answer optimization (new channel, act early)

ChatGPT/Perplexity/Google AI Overviews increasingly answer "how do I
calculate X" directly. To get cited:
- FAQ schema on every page (done).
- Clear, quotable formula statements ("Break-even units = fixed costs ÷
  (price − variable cost)").
- Structured, crawlable HTML — keep the site fast and schema-valid.

## Monetization activation thresholds

| Trigger | Action |
|---|---|
| Site verified in Search Console | Apply for affiliate programs (high-yield savings, business banking, accounting software) and swap placeholder links for real ones |
| ~5K pageviews/mo | Apply for Google AdSense; drop code into the AdSlot component |
| ~50K pageviews/mo | Apply for Mediavine Journey / Raptive (10x AdSense RPMs) |
| Consistent traffic on one category | Launch a $9–19/mo pro tool in that category (e.g. freelance invoicing, rental property analyzer) |

## Metrics that matter

- **Weekly**: indexed pages, impressions, clicks (Search Console).
- **Monthly**: pageviews, top 5 landing pages, revenue per 1K views.
- **Ignore**: social followers, total keywords, vanity traffic spikes.

## 90-day targets

- Day 7: Search Console verified, 20 pages submitted.
- Day 30: 50 pages live, first impressions in Search Console.
- Day 60: 100 pages, first 1K monthly clicks, AdSense approved.
- Day 90: first affiliate payout or $50+ ad revenue, embeddable widget shipped.

## What we will NOT do

- No paid ads until organic unit economics are proven (ads for an ad-funded
  site is lighting money on fire).
- No link-buying or PBNs — one manual penalty kills the whole model.
- No AI-slop content — every page carries real math and real explanations.
